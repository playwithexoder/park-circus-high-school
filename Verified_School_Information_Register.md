# Verified School Information Register

**Purpose:** This document serves as the single source of truth for all factual information regarding Park Circus High School (H.S.). 

To prevent the publication of false or conflicting information, every piece of data must be classified according to the following hierarchy before being used in the digital platform:

## Classification Hierarchy

1. **Government verified:** Confirmed by official government or educational board databases (highest priority).
2. **School verified:** Confirmed directly by authorized school leadership.
3. **Historical:** Found in archival records; useful for context but not to be published as current operational data without re-verification.
4. **Pending verification:** Observed on physical signage, unofficial websites, or legacy materials. Must not be published publicly until verified.
5. **Do not publish:** Known inaccurate, deprecated, or conflicting information.
